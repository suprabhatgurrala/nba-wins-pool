## Creating a Pool {#create}

Choose **Create a pool** from the home page, or **New Pool** on the [pools list](/pools). Fill in the form:

- **Name**: What your group will see, such as "My Cool Pool".
- **Unique ID**: A short identifier used in your pool's link, filled in from the name. It can be up to 20 characters of lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens.
- **Description** (optional): A line about the pool, such as "Annual NBA wins pool with friends".
- **Season**: The season to start with.
- **Season Rules** (optional): House rules and prizes for that season, up to 500 characters.

When you create the pool, you land on its first season page, ready to [set up the season](#setup).

## Setting Up a Season {#setup}

A new season starts with a short setup checklist on its page: add **participants**, then configure the [auction settings](/docs/auction#settings).

Use **Manage Rosters** to add, rename, or remove participants. These can be changed until the auction is started. You need at least 2 participants, but pools work best with 5 to 7.

### Returning for another season

On your pool's home page, click **+ Create** next to the new season, then open it from the list.

Use **Import from Season** to copy last season's participants, then adjust with Manage Rosters and configure your auction.

## Following a Season {#following}

Once everyone has teams, the season page follows game results and updates the standings as wins come in. No daily changes are required from participants. It has three tabs:

- **Standings**: The leaderboard, plus the **wins race**, a chart of how each participant's total has changed over time.
- **Projections**: Projections estimate who will have the most wins by the end of the season. Later in the season, simulations also estimate each participant's odds of winning.
- **Games**: The Games tab lets you see a day's NBA games and which owners have which teams. It also shows TV channels and estimated win probabilities based on Vegas odds.

![Season page Standings tab with the leaderboard and wins race chart](season-standings.webp 'Standings tab')
![Season page Games tab showing a finished playoff game](season-games.webp 'Games tab')

Use the **Share** button in the side menu to share your standings and brag. Links shared to chat apps such as Discord show a preview card. There is also a Discord bot: its **/wins_pool_standings** command posts a pool's standings in your server.

![Season page side menu with the Edit and Share buttons at the top](season-share.webp 'The Share button in the side menu')

## Pool History {#history}

A pool's home page shows a list of all your pool's seasons, with each season's champion. Select one to jump to its standings.

**Participant Stats** shows how each participant has done across seasons. Select a participant to see their own history, including their season results and top contributing teams.

<details>
<summary>How average wins and average projected wins work</summary>

**Average wins** is adjusted to your most recent season's settings if your pool has had different numbers of teams per roster over its history, so seasons can be compared fairly.

**Average projected wins** is the average number of wins projected for a participant's teams at draft time, showing who did best in the moment of the draft. It is adjusted for differing numbers of teams per roster in the same way.

</details>
