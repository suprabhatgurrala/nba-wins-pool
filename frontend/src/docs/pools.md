## Overview

A **pool** is the group taking part in the competition. Each participant gets a few teams through an auction.

Your teams make up your **roster**. When one of them wins a game, that win is added to your score. The standings rank participants by the combined wins of every team on their roster.

A pool can continue across multiple years. Each **season** has its own rosters, standings, and optional house rules or prizes.

Unlike fantasy sports which typically require recurring time every day or every week, wins pools are an alternative that let's you simply root for teams to win games without having to worry about setting lineups or individual player stats.

This app supports a wins pool for the NBA, but other leagues and sports might be added in the future.

## Scoring

For an NBA wins pool, Regular-season, NBA Cup, Play-In Tournament, and Playoff wins all contribute to the scoring. Currently they all count the same, though custom weighting or multipliers may be added in the future. The final score does not matter, and there are no player stats or complicated scoring categories to manage.

## Payouts

The app doesn't handle money. Each group decides what, if anything, is at stake: a payout for the top finishers, or just bragging rights.

Because nothing is left to manage after the draft, some participants may be out of the running before the season ends. To keep everyone invested, consider adding side prizes. A few ideas:

- The roster that owns the NBA MVP
- The roster with the highest-scoring player in a single game
- The roster whose teams improved the most from last season
- The roster that owns the NBA Cup champion
- The roster with the most wins at a milestone, such as the All-Star break, the end of the regular season, or the start of the playoffs

Don't stop at these ideas. Dreaming up side prizes together is part of the fun.

## Drafting Teams

Teams are drafted with the built-in auction. Everyone starts with the same budget and bids on the teams they want. The winning teams are then added to each participant's roster. An auction lets every participant bid on every team, instead of draft position deciding who gets the best ones.
