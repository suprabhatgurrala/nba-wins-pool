## Why an Auction?

In a snake draft, draft position determines too much of who gets the best teams and will often result in unbalanced rosters. An auction avoids that: every participant can bid on every team, and a limited budget turns the draft into a series of trade-offs, since money spent on one team can't be spent on another.

Each participant chooses their own strategy. You can spend big on the preseason favorites and fill out the rest of your roster with whatever is left, or spread your budget across several mid-tier teams and bet that some of them will outperform expectations. For tips on bidding, see [Auction strategy](/docs/auction-strategy).

## Auction Settings {#settings}

Three settings shape how the auction plays out. Use **Edit** to change them until the auction is started.

- **Teams per Participant**: How many teams each participant owns. By default, the 30 teams are divided as evenly as possible, but you can choose fewer to leave more teams undrafted, which adds strategy to the auction.
- **Starting Budget**: The amount each participant has to spend across the whole auction. $200 is typical for fantasy auction drafts, but any amount works. Avoid a very small budget: with too few dollars to go around, bids can't separate the values of different teams.
- **Min Bid Increment**: The minimum opening bid, and the minimum amount each new bid must raise the current bid by. $1 is typical for auction drafts. A larger increment speeds up bidding, but if it is too large, bids can't separate the values of different teams.

## Starting the Auction {#starting}

When your group is ready to draft, open the side menu and choose **Start Auction**.

Once the auction has started, everyone opens the link and chooses **Participate** in the side menu to join as their name. Joined participants can nominate teams and bid. Use **Change** to switch names or **Spectate** to just watch. There are no logins, so joining works on the honor system.

## Nominating Teams {#nominating}

To nominate a team, click its row in the valuation table, or the **+** button next to its name. Check who is nominating, enter an opening bid, and choose **Nominate**. The team then goes up for bidding.

![Nominate Team dialog showing the team, its expected wins and auction value, who is nominating, and an opening bid](auction-nominate.webp 'Nominating a team')

Only one team can be open at a time, so the previous team must be closed before the next nomination.

The order of nominations affects draft strategy, so it's best to follow a snake order: each round goes through the participants in order, then the next round reverses (for example, 1-2-3-4, then 4-3-2-1). The app does not enforce nomination order, so your group needs to keep track of whose turn it is.

## Bidding on a Team {#bidding}

A nominated team becomes the open lot. To bid, enter an amount or select **Min** or **Max** then choose **Submit Bid**. The minimum bid must beat the current high bid by at least the minimum increment. The maximum bid is capped so you keep enough budget to fill your remaining roster spots.

![Open lot for the New York Knicks with the current bid, a bid amount field, Min and Max buttons, and a Submit Bid button](auction-bidding.webp 'Placing a bid on the open lot')
![Auction room with an open lot, the activity feed, and each participant's won teams and remaining budget](auction-room.webp 'The auction room: open lot, activity feed, and participants')

When bidding is finished, anyone in the room can close the lot, so your group decides when that is. The app shows how long it has been since the last bid, but it doesn't enforce time limits. The high bidder wins the team at the final price, and the next team can be nominated.

![Auction side menu during the draft with a Complete Auction button at the top and a Close Lot button under the current lot](auction-close.webp 'Close a lot or complete the auction from the side menu')

## Completing the Auction

Once your group is done drafting, close any open lot and choose **Complete Auction** in the side menu. The auction becomes read-only, and each participant's teams and final prices are added to the pool automatically. You can then go back to the season page and start tracking results.
